# Shareable numbers

_Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD_

Your results are yours to talk about. Your employer's internal metrics usually aren't. Sort them before you're in a room improvising.

| Result | Exact figure | Shareable? | What to say if not |
|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. First-year revenue of a launch | | Public / On my resume / Internal only | e.g. "Beat the first-year goal by about a third" |
| | | | |

## Rules of thumb

- If it appeared in a press release, earnings call, case study, or public talk, it's usually fine.
- Percentages and ratios ("tripled," "cut launch time in half") are usually safer than revenue, bookings, or customer counts.
- If you're unsure, use the relative version. Interviewers care about the size of the change and your role in it, not the exact dollar figure.
- Don't share anything covered by an NDA, including from previous interview take-homes.
