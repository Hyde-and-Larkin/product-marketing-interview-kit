## Stories

Copy this block per story.

### [Story name]

**Hook:** one line you'd use to trigger the whole story in your head.

**Impact first:** the business result, up front, in one or two sentences.

**The challenge:** why it was hard, and what was at stake for the company.

**What I did:** two or three moves, in order. Use "I" for your decisions and "we" for the team's work.

**What changed:** the result again, plus the lasting effect (process adopted, strategy shifted, team grew).

**Key moment:** the specific scene that makes it real (a meeting, a quote, a decision point).
