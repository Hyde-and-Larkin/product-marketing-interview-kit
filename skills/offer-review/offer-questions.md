# Offer and equity questions

Ask these once there's an offer (or late in the process). Not financial or legal advice; see `skills/offer-review`.

## Cash

- What's the base salary, and where does it sit in the band for this level?
- What's the target bonus? How has it actually paid out the last two years?
- Is there a signing bonus?
- When are compensation reviews, and how are raises decided?

## Level and scope

- What level is this, and how does leveling work here?
- What would it take to reach the next level, and how long does that typically take?
- How are leveling, calibration, and pay equity reviewed as the company grows?

## Equity: all companies

- What type of equity: RSUs or options?
- How many units, and what's the current value used to size the grant?
- Vesting schedule and cliff?
- How do refresh grants work, and how often?
- Is there any acceleration on acquisition (single- or double-trigger)?

## Equity: private companies

- What's the current 409A valuation and strike price?
- How many shares are outstanding, fully diluted? (So you can calculate your percentage.)
- What's the most recent preferred price?
- What's the liquidation preference structure? Any participating preferred?
- How long is the post-departure exercise window?
- Is early exercise allowed?
- Have there been any secondary sales or tender offers for employees?

## Everything else

- Start date flexibility?
- Remote, location, or travel expectations in writing?
- Learning budget, equipment, and other benefits worth knowing about?
- Anything in the offer letter or agreements (non-compete, IP assignment) worth reviewing before signing?
