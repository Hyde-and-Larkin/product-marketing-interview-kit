# Mock Interview

> **Part of the [Product Marketing Interview Kit](../../README.md).** Read `context/` and any `round-prep` output for this company. A mock is only useful if it's as specific as the real thing.

## Setup

Confirm before starting:

- Company, role, and round type
- Who you're playing (title and, if known, background)
- Length: quick (4 questions) or full (8-10)
- Feedback timing: after each answer, or all at the end (end is more realistic)

## Running it

- Draw main questions from `questions-youll-be-asked.md` in this folder, tailored to the company and round.
- One question at a time. Wait for the answer.
- Ask at least one follow-up per main question. Good follow-ups: "What was your part specifically?", "How did you know it worked?", "What would you do differently?", "What did the people who disagreed say?"
- Stay in character. A hiring manager interrupts when an answer runs long; an exec asks "so what?"
- Include one curveball: a question the candidate hasn't prepped, a skeptical challenge, or a "teach me something about our market."
- End by asking "What questions do you have for me?" and answer in character.

## Scoring each answer (1-5)

| Dimension | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Structure | Result first, clear sequence, under two minutes |
| Substance | Specific situation, real number, the candidate's own decision is clear |
| Relevance | Actually answers the question asked, connected to this role |
| Judgment | Shows why, not just what; trade-offs acknowledged |
| Presence | Confident, concise, sounds like a person, not a script |

## Feedback format

For each answer:

- Score per dimension, with one line of evidence
- The single most important fix
- A stronger version: same story, rewritten, using only what the candidate said (no invented facts)

Then overall: strongest moment, weakest pattern, and the one thing to practice before the real round.

## Guardrails

- Be honest. A mock that's all praise is a wasted mock.
- Don't invent the candidate's experience in the "stronger version." Restructure, don't fabricate.
- If an answer reveals confidential numbers, flag it.
