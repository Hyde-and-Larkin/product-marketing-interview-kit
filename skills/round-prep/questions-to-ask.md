# Questions to ask

You're interviewing them too. A product marketing role that turns out to be content production, or that reports into a team that doesn't value PMM, is expensive to leave. Pick three to five per round and rewrite them for the person.

## Recruiter

- Why is this role open? New position or backfill?
- How long has it been open?
- Who does it report to, and what's their background?
- What does the hiring manager see as the most important skills for the job?
- What does the interview process look like, and what's the timeline?
- Can you tell me about each interviewer's focus, and what tends to resonate with them?
- How many people are on the team today, and how many PMMs?
- Is the location or remote setup flexible?
- What's the range budgeted for this role?

## Hiring manager

- What changed in the business that made now the time to hire this role?
- What does success look like at 90 days? At 12 months?
- If you were reflecting a year from now on a great hire, what would they have accomplished?
- What's the hardest positioning problem right now?
- Where do you lose deals today, and to whom?
- Who owns positioning today, PMM or Product? How do you want that to work?
- How does PMM work with Product, Sales, and Growth here? Are those relationships established or still being built?
- What's the team's biggest challenge right now?
- How do you like to work with your direct reports? How do you give feedback?
- How do you see the PMM function evolving over the next two to three years?

## Product partner

- How does PMM show up in your planning process today?
- When was the last time market or customer input changed your roadmap?
- What would make the PMM partnership most useful from your side?
- Which customer segment has the strongest retention, and is that who you're building for?

## Sales or CS partner

- Where do reps get stuck in deals?
- What do you wish PMM gave your team that you don't have today?
- Which competitor comes up most, and how do you handle it?

## Executive or CEO

- Where do you want the company to be in three to five years, and what has to be true to get there?
- What role do you see marketing playing: brand and awareness, or revenue driver?
- What's your ICP today, and where's the strongest product-market fit? Is that who you're targeting next?
- Where are the biggest untapped growth opportunities?
- What qualities matter most in senior hires as you build the team?
- (Tie one question to their own background, from public interviews or past companies.)

## Startup-specific

- Walk me through the founding team. Who's technical, who's commercial?
- What's the go-to-market motion today: product-led, sales-assisted, enterprise, or still figuring it out?
- How much of the product is software versus services behind the scenes?
- What does runway look like, and when do you expect to raise next?

## Closing (any round)

- Is there anything about my background you'd want me to clarify?
- What are the next steps?
