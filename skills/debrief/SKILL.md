# Debrief

> **Part of the [Product Marketing Interview Kit](../../README.md).** Updates `context/pipeline.md` and can feed improvements back into `context/story-bank.md`.

Do this within an hour. By tomorrow you'll remember how it felt, not what was said.

## Ask the candidate

1. Who did you talk to, and how long?
2. What did they ask? (as close to verbatim as possible)
3. Which answers landed? Which felt weak?
4. What did you learn about the role, team, or company that you didn't know?
5. Any red flags or green flags?
6. What did they say about next steps and timing?
7. Did you promise to send anything?

## Output

```
## [Company]: [Round] with [Interviewer], [date]

**Questions asked:**

**Landed:**
**Weak (and the fix):**

**What I learned about the role:**
**Flags:** 🟢 ... 🔴 ...

**Next step and timing:**
**Owed:**

**Story bank updates:** (new follow-ups to drill, stories to sharpen)
```

## Thank-you note

Short, specific, sent the same day. Three or four sentences:

- Thanks, with one specific thing from the conversation
- One line connecting it to what you'd bring
- Any follow-up you promised, attached or linked

No restating your resume, no long recap.

## Pipeline update

Update the company's row in `pipeline.md`: stage, next step, date, status. If the process ends, fill in "why / why not." Over time, that column shows where you stall and what to fix.

## Guardrails

- Notes about interviewers stay in the private copy.
- Add new questions to the candidate's private question log, not the public bank, unless they're generic and stripped of company detail.
