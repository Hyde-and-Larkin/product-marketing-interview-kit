# Contributing

Improvements are welcome, especially from people who've just been through a PMM loop.

## Good contributions

- Questions you were asked that aren't in the bank (generic wording, no company names)
- A better way to prep for a round type
- A fix to a skill that gave weak or generic advice

## Ground rules

- **No confidential interview material.** Don't submit a company's take-home prompt, internal documents, interviewer names, or anything you agreed not to share.
- No personal comp details.
- Keep skills tool-neutral: plain Markdown.

Fork, change, and open a pull request with a sentence on why. Or open an issue with the suggestion.
