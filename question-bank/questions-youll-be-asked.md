# Questions you'll be asked

Collected from real product marketing interview loops, from IC roles to Director, at companies from seed stage to public. Grouped by what the interviewer is actually trying to learn.

## About you

*Testing: are you clear, credible, and pointed at this role?*

- Tell me about yourself / walk me through your background.
- Why do you want to make a change?
- Why this role, and why now?
- Why this company?
- What's your edge for this role?
- What would your last manager say you're world-class at? What would they say you need to work on?
- What's your biggest weakness?
- What are your top three values?
- What are you looking for in your next manager?

## Product marketing judgment

*Testing: do you understand what PMM is for?*

- What is the role of product marketing? What's its value?
- What did product marketing look like where you worked before?
- How do you measure the success of product marketing?
- What good product do you think is marketed poorly? How would you fix it?
- How do you develop positioning and messaging, especially for a technical product?
- How do you approach competitive positioning when many players offer similar capabilities?
- How do you approach audience segmentation and customer insight?
- How would you size an opportunity or a market?
- Tell me about a time you repositioned a product or changed messaging strategy.
- How do you balance messaging for business buyers versus technical implementers?

## Launches and go-to-market

*Testing: can you run the work end to end, and does it move numbers?*

- Walk me through your last product launch.
- Describe a successful launch. What made it successful?
- Walk me through a GTM strategy that mattered at the company level.
- What's your approach to go-to-market for a brand-new product?
- How would you go to market with two distinct products without confusing the market?
- How do you approach international expansion for a product with regulatory complexity?
- How do you approach sales enablement for a complex product?
- What would you do differently if you ran that launch again?
- What was the timeline from concept to first revenue?

## Working with product, sales, and leadership

*Testing: will people want to work with you, and will you have influence?*

- How do you work with product managers? What's your style?
- How do you influence the roadmap without direct authority?
- Tell me about a time PMM insight changed a major product or GTM decision.
- How do you handle disagreement when Product, Sales, and Growth want different things?
- How do you prioritize competing requests from Product, Sales, and leadership?
- How do you build relationships with new partners?
- How do you work with and communicate to executive leadership?
- How do you get executive buy-in for a new approach?
- How do you make sure experiments are well designed, not random A/B tests?

## Leading PMM teams (manager and Director roles)

*Testing: can you build and run the function?*

- What's your management philosophy?
- How do you structure a high-performing PMM team? What do you look for when hiring?
- Generalist or specialist PMMs?
- How do you measure individual PMM performance?
- Give me a specific example of developing someone on your team.
- How do you handle underperformance?
- How do you make sure PMM has a voice at the executive level?
- How many people would you be comfortable managing?
- How would you build PMM from scratch, or in a messy high-growth environment?
- What's been your biggest challenge scaling a team?

## The business and the role

*Testing: do you have a point of view on us?*

- How would you approach the first 90 days?
- What do you see as our biggest challenge this year?
- How would you position [our product] to a skeptical buyer?
- How would you split time between two products?
- Where do you see cross-sell opportunities?
- How do you think about speed versus quality in a high-velocity culture?
- How do you think about trust, safety, or brand risk in our go-to-market?
- What's your experience with AI, and how do you use it in your work?

## Logistics

- What are your compensation expectations? (see `skills/offer-review`)
- What's your timeline? Are you interviewing elsewhere?
